The problem

Nobody knows which quotes are still open.

Contractors, consultants and industrial suppliers send quotations and then lose track of them across email and Excel. Nobody knows which quotes are still open, how much money is sitting in them, or which client has gone quiet for three weeks.

The sales head finds out only when the order goes to a competitor.

What I do about it

Three small things, set up in a week.

  • Enquiry intake to tracker sheet

  • Quotation chase board

    Every open quote sits on one board, so you see what is still live and who has gone quiet.

    A short Monday email of new project leads, so the week starts with a list and not a search.
